# Classification change 22 Nov 2026: per-trial labels from reviewed SEC filings

From the weekly run of 23 Nov 2026, 50 trials take their label from a reviewed passage in the sponsor's own SEC filing instead of from the registry stop reason. The registry text of each of them gives no cause (or an unresolved one); the filing names it. Source in the data: `classification_source = SEC_FILING_REVIEWED`; the decisions are in `data/sec_evidence_decisions.csv`, the review in `docs/sec_evidence_review_2026-11-22.md`.

## How the labels were made

- Candidate passages: EDGAR full-text search per trial (`scripts/sources/sec_stop_evidence.py`), only filings of the sponsor or a mapped parent (`data/sec_sponsor_map.csv`), matched on the tested drug (rule 8: the difference between the arms, never the shared backbone) and on the trial's identity.
- Review: all 101 candidates were reviewed with AI assistance (Claude), not by a clinician. 50 were accepted; the 51 rejected ones are listed in the review doc.
- A filing that speaks of the drug's whole programme ("development discontinued") rather than of this trial is accepted only when this trial was running at the time and the filing gives the cause. Those trials carry rule `sec.programme_filing` in their evidence, so a programme stop stays distinguishable from a trial-level statement (`sec.trial_filing`).
- Confidence is MEDIUM for all 50. A filing is the sponsor's account to investors, not a regulatory finding.
- The trial page shows the quoted sentence, the form, the filing date and a link to the filing.
