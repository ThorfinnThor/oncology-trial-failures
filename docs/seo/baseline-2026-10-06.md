## Protection list

The machine-readable protection list is `docs/seo/protected-urls.csv`. It is deliberately small and explicit: pilot, controls, watch pages, and the previously inspected trial page. It is not a claim that every historically valuable URL has been discovered. A complete 90-day export, backlink dataset, and product-usage dataset are still required before any sitewide exclusion decision.

## Current decisions

- No sitewide `noindex`, redirect, canonical, or removal decision is made by this baseline.
- The Ophthalmology pilot is already live because Cloudflare automatically deploys merges to `main`; future merges must be treated as production releases.
- The next measurement actions are a single URL inspection/indexing request and the 7/28/56-day reviews, after the remaining audit work is agreed.
