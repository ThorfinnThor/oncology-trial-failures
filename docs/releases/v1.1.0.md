Second archived release of the Clinical Trial Failures dataset and pipeline (snapshot of 23 Nov 2026).

- **24,132** terminated, suspended and withdrawn trials from ClinicalTrials.gov, each classified by the sponsor's stated stop reason, with the evidence it rests on: biological failure 2,479 (efficacy/futility, safety, benefit–risk), mixed causes 232, non-biological 14,184, not a failure 1,613, cause not stated 1,991, unresolved 3,633. Classifier 2.7.7.
- **New: causes beyond the registry text.** Where the registry gives no cause, reviewed per-trial decisions (AI-assisted review, not clinicians) supply it, each linked to its source: the sponsor's SEC filing (50 trials), the trial's own publication (9, PMID and our summary) and the EU Clinical Trials Information System's coded early-termination category (13). Searched weekly from now on.
- **Classifier 2.6 → 2.7.7:** "nobody enrolled" is no longer a recruitment cause on its own; a partner withdrawing is support withdrawal; a changed standard of care is a design cause; denials with an article ("not due to the lack of efficacy") stay denials. Every change is listed with each label it moved (`docs/classification_change_*.md`).
- **Endpoint labels:** trials with posted results are read for whether the sponsor's primary superiority analysis met or missed, only against a threshold the record states or every conventional threshold agrees on; otherwise not assessable.
- **Validation:** held-out sample (n=600, blind annotation): biological-failure precision 95.5 %, recall 95.3 %; second model family 98.2 % agreement on biological vs. not; one clinician confirmed 101 of 103 biological labels in a blind oncology review. Details: https://clinicaltrialfailures.com/validation
- Free labelled sample with data dictionary: https://clinicaltrialfailures.com/datasets and https://huggingface.co/datasets/clinical-trial-failures/clinical-trial-failures-sample

The dataset is rebuilt weekly; this release is a fixed snapshot for citation.
