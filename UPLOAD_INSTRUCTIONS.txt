CLINICALTRIALFAILURES.COM - VERSION 70

This package adds three new insight articles:
- A critical review of the patient-hedge argument for clinical trial markets
- A data analysis of other and unknown stop reasons
- A data analysis of regulatory clinical trial stops

UPLOAD STEPS
1. Extract clinicaltrialfailures-three-new-insights-v70-upload.zip.
2. Open your oncology-trial-failures repository on GitHub.
3. Click Add file, then Upload files.
4. Open the extracted upload-three-insights-v70 folder.
5. Drag the web folder into GitHub's upload area.
6. Confirm that GitHub shows these paths:
   web/lib/insights.ts
   web/lib/insightStats.ts
7. Enter this commit message:
   Add three research insight articles (v70)
8. Click Commit changes and wait for Vercel to redeploy.

The dataset-derived facts and tables are recalculated during every site build
after an ingest. Sitemap.xml and llms.txt include the new articles automatically.
No .github files were changed.
