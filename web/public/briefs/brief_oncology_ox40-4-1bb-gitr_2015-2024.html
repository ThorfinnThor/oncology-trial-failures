The 5 stops came from 5 sponsor-asset programmes across 5 sponsors; the largest (Pfizer) contributed 1; removing that programme's trials from both sides leaves 12.5%. Screened alongside 83 other segments in this area, it does not survive a 10% false-discovery correction (q=1, Benjamini-Yekutieli). Method
Denominator: ClinicalTrials.gov interventional Phase 2,3 oncology trials started 2015–2024 that have closed (completed or terminated). Numerator: terminated trials whose registry stop reason is classified as biological (efficacy, safety or benefit–risk) by Classification V2 — held-out precision 95.5%, recall 95.3% (n=600). Drugs are linked to ChEMBL and the NCI Thesaurus; 76% of industry oncology trials in this window carry a resolved drug target, and a trial without one cannot enter a mechanism class. Intervals are Wilson 95%.
Limits
Not a failure rate: trials that completed with negative results are not counted, and programs discontinued after a completed trial do not appear. Stop reasons are sponsor-reported. This is a closed-trial proportion, not a time-to-event analysis: only 38.4% of trials in this segment have closed, and a trial that stops early enters the denominator sooner than one that runs to completion, which can inflate the rate in immature segments. Recent cohorts have fewer closed trials, so their rates are less stable. Research signals, not clinical or investment advice.
