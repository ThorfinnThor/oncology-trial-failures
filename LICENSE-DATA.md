## Source data: the source's own terms

Registry fields come from [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov) (U.S. National
Library of Medicine) and stay under its terms. These include the NCT ID, titles, status, the
sponsor's `why_stopped` text, phases, dates, sponsors, conditions, interventions and
enrolment. This licence does not restrict them, and nothing here limits a right that a source
licence already gives you.

Where other sources contribute fields (for example ChEMBL under CC BY-SA 3.0, or NCI Thesaurus
under CC BY 4.0), those fields keep their source licence. See
[`docs/provenance_and_rights.md`](docs/provenance_and_rights.md).
